# What is AdvantageKit
Advantagekit is the library behind all of our logging: from sending data through `NT4`, to keeping logs and writing files, Advantagekit is how we are able to monitor our robot in real time, and debug/test

In this section, you'll learn how Advantagekit works under the surface, how we use it to send data, and constantly rely on it's real time monitoring capabilities to ensure our robot moves and works how it's supposed to.
